The Name We Grow Into

They crossed the whole dark to GO find others. Tonight they find out what the others grew up calling THEM.
Out past the open door, the crew chases a name they can't quite catch while a whole caravan of not-yet lights comes up the forward road already remembering them — and learns that you don't pick what you mean to someone; the ones you kept a light on for decide that, and they've been deciding it a long, long time.

What this issue is really about

You never really get to choose what you are to someone else. The small kind thing you did and forgot — leaving a light on, saying a soft hello, waiting one more minute — might be the very thing another person built their whole way home around. You find out who you are by what people carry of you. So leave the light on even when no one's looking. Especially then. Someone far ahead is steering by it, and to them you're not small at all.

Named at last by the very lights they'd kept warm — the LAMPLIGHTERS, the ones who go out ahead into the dark and leave a light burning so no one who comes after has to find the way alone — the crew learned the thing under the name: you don't get to choose what you mean to someone; the ones you kept a light on for decide it, and they'd been steering by the crew as their whole sky long before the crew knew they existed. Rivet carved LAMPLIGHTERS as the nineteenth line — not a word beneath the eighteen, but the title OVER all of them, the name every earned word had been spelling out. But the forward road wasn't done delivering. Behind the whole caravan of kept lights that came up the road already remembering the crew, one more light wandered in the dark — not steering by them, not remembering them, asking the oldest retired question of the entire saga: 'Is anyone there?' This one is different: it comes from the FORWARD dark, a someone in the not-yet who grew up with no lamplighter at all, who's asked a hundred times and never once been answered and no longer believes a yes is real. Echo said yes anyway; the little light flickered with hope and pulled back, unable to trust it. On the B-side, Shane reached past the porch switch and the forward switch to a third, further one and lit a road into a forward dark he'd never mapped — for the one who never got a light. Led by Nova (the forward dark's former loneliest light) and the newcomer (who remembers being scared of the warm best of all), the newly-named Lamplighters turn down the long dark road to be the first answer for a someone who's never believed one. Rivet feels a twentieth line glowing — not yet a word — the name for being somebody's first answer when you yourself were once nobody's.
