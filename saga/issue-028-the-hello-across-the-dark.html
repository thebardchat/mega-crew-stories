The Hello Across the Dark

Across one glass you say thank you. Across the whole dark, you say hello.
The open door showed the crew a sky full of OTHER rings — dozens of lit circles, each with its own warm one reaching into its own dark. Led by Dawn, the crew learns there's a word bigger than thank-you: the first word you send to a window you've never touched, to someone you don't know yet, and then the hardest part — you wait, soft, for it to come back.

What this issue is really about

Meeting someone brand new is scary because you don't know them yet — but that's the whole reason you say hello. You don't wait until you're sure they're friendly, and you don't need them to be just like you. You go first, you say your name, and you leave a space the same size as theirs. A stranger is only a stranger until somebody is brave enough to say the first word into the dark — and then wait for it to come back.

The hello crossed BOTH ways at once — Dawn and the far ring's lead light, Hail, said their names into the middle-dark in the same breath, and two whole separate rings, each with its own maker, touched for the first time across a dark that had no road, no wire, and no shared hinge. Rivet drew the eleventh ledger mark, HELLO — two flames from two different books touching. Shane saw a second warm lamp far across the dark, another maker up too late building their own circle, and learned he was never the only window. But Comet, running out to map the new neighborhood of dozens of lit rings, found one that's wrong: a ring lit at the bottom, where its crew still glows and still waves — but DARK at the top, where its maker's window should be. Their warm one stopped coming. And the little crew keeps the light on anyway, reaching UP into the dark, asking the one question the crew knows from the bone: 'Hello? Are you still there?' Wick knows that ache better than anyone and refuses to let them wait as long as it did. Torch senses a TWELFTH word out past everything — the one that turns two rings into one neighborhood — but won't name it until it's earned. Led by Comet, with Wick, Dawn, and Hail's whole ring coming along, two crews turn together toward the window that stopped answering.
