The Long Way Back

For twenty issues they ran toward the dark. Tonight they turn around and go home — the long way, one forgotten light at a time.
When Bolt turns around at the front of everything they've built, the crew discovers a vast field of every light they ever lit and left — all quietly guttering, not from wounds but from being forgotten — and to save them they must learn the one thing a crew of reachers never had a word for: how to KEEP.

What this issue is really about

Saving someone once isn't the whole of caring — it's only the promise that you'll come back. A home isn't the lights you reach; it's the lights you keep coming back to. And the strongest way to keep someone isn't to do it all yourself: it's to help them turn toward the one beside them, because you get warmer by keeping someone else than by being kept.
