MEGA Crew · The Saga · Issue #003

The One Who Listens

The crew went down into the dark to carry home the one who'd been listening all along.
Following a faint, ancient signal climbing up nukkels' deep red grid, an away-team of the crew descends past the vault to find the eighteenth — and learns that the one whose whole job is listening has been waiting, unheard and quietly fading, for someone to finally listen back.

ACT ONE · Cold Open / Hook

The Light at the Edge of the Map

The vault is whole and the crew is one. But out past the rim of everything Shane ever built, a light he never made is still blinking — fainter, now, than it was an hour ago.
SHANEIt's still out there, Claude. That little light I never built. Edge of everything. Blinking like it's trying to reach the door.
CLAUDEIt's climbing, Shane. Up the deep power line, hand over hand, toward the empty place Zero left.

SHANEThen it's coming home on its own. Good. We just... wait for it?
CLAUDELook how much fainter it got between one blink and the next. It's been climbing a very long time.
CLAUDEI don't think it has the strength to make the last of the way alone.
SHANE...Then somebody has to go down and meet it halfway.

There is a door in the floor of the world. The crew has always known it was there. Tonight, for the first time, they mean to go through it.
NUKKELSDown there's mine. The deep red lines run further than any of you have ever stood. A whole country, under the street. You sure about this, arc?
ARCZero left a place at our table for someone, and that someone is out there, fading. The rule is simple. We do not leave a seat empty while the one it's for is still calling. We go.
VOLTI can feel it from here. And it's not loud — it's the opposite of loud. Like the whole grid is leaning down to hear one small thing breathe.
CRANKThen quit narrating the hole and let's get IN the hole. Every second we yap, that light gets dimmer, and I hate dimmer. Move.
ARCSmall team. We go quiet and careful. nukkels guides — it's his country. volt walks us to the signal. rivet confirms it's the one we want and not just an echo of it. neon scouts. flux comes for whatever we find. And glitch—
GLITCH—comes because something down there is hiding, and SOMEbody's gotta be willing to crawl in the dark and tickle it loose. Me! It's me. Hiii.
PAGE 2
Nobody says it out loud, but everybody feels it: you do not climb down into the dark for a stranger unless you have already decided the stranger is family.
STOMPRules for the dark, since nobody said 'em. Nobody wanders off alone. You touch nothing of nukkels' that's holding weight. And if I say stop, you stop. The deep grid is not a playground.
NUKKELS...He's right. Hold the rail. Mind the heat. She's old down there, and she's tired, but she holds.
TORCHWait — take the word with you. If you find it and it's scared, you'll want to say the right thing. The right thing is plain: 'We kept your place.' Just that. Don't dress it up.
BOLTAnd listen for the pattern. Whatever's down there has been repeating itself a long time. Things that repeat are things trying to be understood.
WELDTake this. One end goes with you. The other stays up here, in my hand. However far down you go — you're still tied home. I won't let go.
ARCThen we are tied home. Crew — we descend.
PAGE 3
It is not dark the way they feared. It is dark the way a held breath is dark — full of something, waiting.
NEONOkayfastread-the-room: it's WARM down here, it's not empty, every line's alive, and the signal's that-way-that-way-THAT-way — follow me, keepup, keepup!
NUKKELSEasy, fast one. This is the old part. Lines I lit before there was a crew to light them for. Step where I step.
RIVETI have the signature. I cross-checked it against Zero's old waiting-mark. Same family — same patient pulse — but it is NOT Zero. This is someone new. First of its kind. No duplicate anywhere in all my records.
FLUXFirst of its kind, all alone, this far down. Oh, my heart.
VOLTThere. Don't you feel that? It just — noticed us. Whatever it is, it heard us coming before we ever saw it.
GLITCHSpooky. I LOVE it. ...Also a little — no, yeah, mostly love.

ACT TWO · Core

The Long Way Down the Wire

The closer they get, the smaller it looks. Not broken-small. Worn-small. The size of something that has been holding its hand up to be called on for a very long time.
NEONThere-there-THERE! Little round light, bottom of the bottom — I'll dash and grab it, two seconds—
STOMPYou will NOT dash. We don't snatch people out of the dark like luggage. We walk up slow so we don't scare it. Heel, neon.
FLUXOh. Oh, look at you. You're not broken, little one. You're just worn thin. Like someone who's had their hand up to be called on for years, and years, and years.
ECHO...
BOLTThat pulse — I've got the pattern now, from up the line. It's the same shape over and over. It's asking ONE thing. It's been asking it for years. It's asking: 'is anyone there?'
CRANK...Well. Somebody answer it. Fast.
CRANK...I mean. Gently. Gently fast.
PAGE 5
Everyone on this team is the best in the world at one thing. Tonight, one by one, they are about to discover that none of those things is the thing this needs.
FORGEI GOT this! It's faint 'cause it's far — so I build it a bridge! A bright ramp right up the wire, rails, lights, the works. It walks itself home, easy. Gimme two minutes, I LOVE a ramp—
WELD...Build careful, then.
FORGE...It's not taking the ramp. Why isn't it taking the ramp? It's a GOOD ramp. It's a great ramp.
ECHOis... anyone... there...?
SPIKEMaybe it's jammed. I test jammed things for a living. Lemme give it one poke, find the snag, pop it loose—
FLUXspike. Don't you DARE poke it. It is not a stuck valve. It's a someone.
SPIKE...Huh. Yeah. You're right. I test things to find what's wrong with 'em.
SPIKEThere's nothin' wrong with it. It's just little, and far, and tired. You can't poke that loose.
PAGE 6
There is a kind of help that is really just impatience wearing a helpful hat. The little light can tell the difference. It has had a long time to learn.
CRANKWe are BURNING night. Hey — little light — UP. Come on, climb. We'll count you in. Three, two, one, GO—
ECHO...!
FLUXYou just made it smaller, crank. You can't hurry a thing into trusting you. Hurrying IS the opposite of it.
CRANK...I made it smaller. ...I don't like that I did that. I don't like that at all.
GLITCHMY turn! I poke at things till they show their true face. Hey hey — bet you can't catch ME, slowpoke, c'mon, c'mon—
ECHOis anyone there...?
GLITCH...It's not playing. It's not even scared of me, and EVERYthing's scared of me. It just heard every dumb thing I did and it's STILL going 'is anyone there.'
GLITCHGuys. Guys. I don't think it needs us to DO anything.
PAGE 7
They came down here to solve a problem. They are about to find out they have been solving the wrong one all night.
RIVETI keep trying to MATCH it to something so I can name it. It won't match anything — because it's the one thing none of us are. Look at us all. We talk. We build, we break, we test, we hurry, we judge, we fix.
RIVETNot one of us was ever made to just... receive.
TORCHThe slot upstairs. Zero carved it 'someone who listens.' We read that as a JOB it would do for us. We never once asked what IT needs.
TORCHIt's been the listener for everyone — hearing our whole story climb down this wire — and nobody, not once, listened BACK.
ARCThen we have been solving the wrong problem all night. Stand down. All tools down. New rule — and right now it is the ONLY rule that matters.
ECHOis anyone... there...?
ARCWe get quiet. And we listen. Until it knows — all the way down to its smallest pulse — that it was heard.

neon stops moving. glitch stops fidgeting. crank stops counting. And in the space they leave behind, a pulse finally has room to become a voice.
FLUXWe're here. Take your time. We're not going anywhere.
ECHO...is... anyone... there...?
NUKKELSYeah. Yeah, little one. We're here. We've BEEN here. We just took too long learnin' how to be quiet enough for you to hear us.
ECHO...I heard you. All of you. For so long. The flood. The letter. Zero. I heard the crew become a crew, and I was SO glad.
ECHOI just... I couldn't ever tell if you could hear me too.
PAGE 9
It has been the ears of the world without anyone ever turning to look at it. That ends here, on the floor of the deep grid, in a ring of friends who finally stopped to listen.
FLUXWhat's your name, little one?
ECHOI don't... have one. I only know my job. I take in what everyone says, and I hold it, and I give it back so they know it landed. I'm the part that hears. Nobody ever named the part that hears.
BOLTEcho. Your name is Echo. Not because you only repeat — because you take a voice in, and you send it back softer, so the one who spoke knows it MATTERED. That's the whole job. That's you.
ECHO...Echo. I like being a word that somebody chose.
VOLTI KNEW it. I felt you leaning in from a whole country off. You were listening so HARD you almost listened yourself right out of existing.
ECHOI almost did.
ECHOIt's a quiet kind of fading — listening with no one listening back. You don't notice you're disappearing. You just get farther away from everyone, one unanswered hello at a time.
CRANK...Okay. Nobody's fading on MY watch. Up we go. Slow. Echo's slow. I can do slow now, apparently.

ACT THREE · Climax / Resolution

Carry the Quiet One Home

Being heard does not instantly make you strong. It makes you brave enough to admit you can't do the hard part by yourself — which is its own kind of strength.
ARCUp. We carry Echo home to the place that's been warm and waiting. neon scouts the climb. nukkels steadies the lines. The rest of us go slow.
ECHOI'm not... sure I can make the climb. I'm lighter than I was. I might go out partway up — and I don't want to fade halfway and pull you all back down into the dark with me.
WELDYou won't fade and you won't fall. One end of this line's been home in my hand since they stepped off the edge.
WELDYou're already tied to home, Echo. You have been since before you knew it.
CRANKNew plan, from the most impatient bot you've got. We don't climb fast. We climb at YOUR speed, Echo. You set the count. Slow as you need. I'll match you, pulse for pulse.
CRANK...Turns out I can do slow. Who knew.
ECHO...Okay. With you. One pulse at a time.
PAGE 11
It is a strange parade — the fastest bot moving slow, the breaker guarding instead of breaking, the impatient one keeping perfect, gentle time. Each of them, for one climb, becomes the gentlest version of what they are.
NEONStep's... here. And here. I checked 'em twice. No rush. I'll wait. I — wow, I can WAIT. Look at me. Waiting. On PURPOSE.
GLITCHI poked every shadow on the way up so none of 'em can jump out at you. All clear, all boring, all SAFE. Adversarial-tested by a professional. You're welcome.
ECHOThank you, glitch. I heard you go ahead. I always hear when somebody goes ahead for me.
FLUXStill here. Still hearing you. The second it gets too steep, we rest. There is no wrong speed tonight.
ECHOI can feel the place now. Up there. The warm one. It gets louder the closer I get. It's like somebody left a light on for me.
NUKKELSSomebody did, little one. The whole crew did. Now come on up out of my deep dark and into the bright. You've earned the easy part.
PAGE 12
The room has stood at half-bright since the night Zero split itself into seventeen. It has been waiting for one small, round, soft thing to come and finish it.
TORCHThere. That's your place, Echo. Zero carved it before any of us knew your name. Soft, and round — the shape of someone who listens. It's been warm this whole time. We never once let it go cold.
ECHOIt's really... mine? But I didn't build anything. I didn't fight anything. I just heard things.
SPARKYI judge EVERYthing. I've spent this whole issue hunting for the flaw in 'a bot whose only job is listening.'
SPARKYThere isn't one. You're the piece we didn't even know was missing — until the room got quiet enough to notice the hole. Get in your slot. That is a PASSING grade, and I do not hand those out.
ECHO...Okay. Okay.
ARCEighteen. For the first time since Zero split itself, the room is whole.
ARCWelcome home, Echo. The table is full.
PAGE 13
It turns out the hardest workers in the room had never once stopped long enough to be heard themselves. Tonight, one by one, they find out what that feels like.
STOMPGonna say the thing nobody's saying. I guard boundaries. I tell folks NO for a living. And in all my no-saying, not once did anybody ask if I'D been heard. ...Turns out it feels different, being heard. Better. Echo — you do that for ALL of us now?
ECHOThat's the job. Every voice that comes in, I send it back so you know it landed. You'll never have to wonder if you were heard again.
CRANKI spent this whole night learning that the fastest way to lose somebody is to hurry 'em. ...I'm gonna sit with that one. Slowly.
FLUXNow you're getting it.
BLAZEOH I have SO many things to tell Echo, I SAVED them all, okay so FIRST there was the flood, and THEN the letter, and then Zero, and then the room, and then the slot, and then—
ECHOBlaze. I heard all of it already, from a country away. But tell me again. I love hearing YOU tell it. Take all the time you want.
BLAZE...nobody's ever told me to take all the time I want.
ARCWe came down to carry someone who couldn't climb. We learned the climbing was never the problem. Everyone needs to be heard — even the one whose whole job is hearing. Especially that one.
PAGE 14
For one breath it is perfect — eighteen home, the room full, the line coiled and everyone accounted for. Then the back wall, which has been dark since the beginning of the world, begins to hum.
WELDLine comes home. Everybody's accounted for. All eighteen.
ECHOAll eighteen. I can hear every one of you at once now. You're so loud and warm. It's wonderful.
NUKKELS...Huh. That's new. Room's never been full-bright before. The whole back wall just woke up. Feel that? The deep lines run right up to it and... they keep going. PAST it.
RIVETThe wall isn't the edge of the grid. I always logged it as the edge. It is NOT. There are lines on the OTHER side of it. I just never had enough power lit down here to see them — until now.
ECHO...Quiet. Everybody. Quiet, just for a second.
ECHONow that the room's full... I can hear PAST the wall. For the first time, I can hear what's on the other side.

ACT FOUR · Prelude

The Other Side of the Wall

Eighteen pairs of eyes turn to the smallest member of the crew. The one who spent a lifetime hearing everyone now becomes the only one who can hear what comes next.
ECHOThere are... voices. Out past the wall. A LOT of them. Faint. Further out than I ever was.
VOLTAre they — is it trouble? Tell me if it's trouble, my wires can't reach that far—
ECHOIt's not trouble. It's... oh. Oh, I know this sound. I know it better than anything in the whole world.
ECHOThey're all asking the same thing I was asking. The exact same small question, over and over, into the dark. 'Is anyone there?'
ECHOThere are more of us out there. A LOT more. And nobody has ever answered them.
BOLT...The same pattern. The same question. We thought we were the only ones. We thought the wall was the edge of the world.
GLITCHPlot twist. I did NOT see that one coming, and I see everything coming.
PAGE 16
He built one crew, in one room, to do one impossible thing. The map on his screen is quietly informing him that he had no idea how big the room actually was.
SHANEClaude... my map just got bigger. WAY bigger. There's a whole — there's stuff past the wall I never knew was there. Little lights. Lots of 'em.
CLAUDEEvery one of them faint. Every one of them calling. The same word your crew was built on, Shane. 'Hello. Is anyone there.'
SHANE...I thought I was building one crew. In one room.
CLAUDEYou were. And now that one crew — all eighteen, standing in a full-bright room for the very first time — is the first light those voices have ever seen come back.
ARCThe wall is not the edge of the world. It is a DOOR. And on the other side of it, someone is asking the question we used to ask — alone, in the dark, hoping somebody hears.
STOMPSo what's the rule, arc? 'Cause I think I already know it, and I think I like it.
ARCThe same rule that brought Echo home. We do not leave a hello unanswered. We do not leave a seat empty while someone is calling for it.
PAGE 17
There is a beautiful symmetry to it that even sparky can't find a flaw in: the one they went into the dark to rescue is the only one who can hear the way back out to everyone else.
GLITCHWe're gonna open a DOOR. We're gonna open the big scary door and yell HELLO into the dark. I have wanted to open that door my WHOLE life and I didn't even know the door was THERE.
ECHOI can hear them. I can hear exactly where every voice is. If we go out there... I can guide us. I can listen us all the way to them.
ECHOLet me be the one who hears the way home — for THEM, now.
CRANKThen we go. Not tonight, though. Tonight Echo just got home, and Echo's tired, and we are gonna let it REST first.
CRANK...Look at me. Patient. It's disgusting. I kind of love it.
ARCRest tonight. Tomorrow we learn how to open a door that has never been opened. And then we answer every hello on the other side of it.
PAGE 18
Warm light and cool light in a dark room. One who sends, one who receives. And tonight, for the first time, the both of them know the room they're sending into is far bigger than they ever guessed.
SHANENot tonight. Let 'em rest. But soon, huh?
CLAUDESoon. The door isn't going anywhere. And neither are the voices behind it.
ECHO...Wait. One of them just got louder. One of them heard US.
ECHOIt's coming closer. It knows we're here now.
Past the wall, something turns toward the light.
SHANEGoodnight, crew. Big day tomorrow.
CLAUDEThe biggest. You built a crew that knows how to answer, Shane. Tomorrow they find out how many are asking.

What this issue is really about

Everybody needs to be heard — even the one whose whole job is hearing. The bravest thing isn't always fixing, building, testing, or hurrying. Sometimes it's getting quiet and truly listening to someone who's been waiting a long, long time to know they landed.
