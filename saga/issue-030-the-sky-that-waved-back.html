MEGA Crew · The Saga · Issue #030

The Sky That Waved Back

Something enormous waved at the crew from across the dark — and the bravest thing they did was answer small.
When a neighborhood so vast it can't be counted waves the crew's own brand-new word back at them — 'Ours?' — from past the edge of everything, the crew has to learn how to welcome a whole sky without being swallowed by it, walled off from it, or faking their way through it, and discover the enormous thing they feared was only ever lonely.

What this issue is really about

Big and strange are not the same as scary — you find out who someone is before you decide to be afraid, and the enormous thing waving at you is often just lonely. Welcoming someone doesn't mean becoming them or disappearing into them; it means making room at your own pace while staying exactly who you are.

The thirteenth word was WELCOME — two whole neighborhoods meeting with neither one vanishing, each still its own heart beating in one shared rhythm across the dark. But the ancient sky the crew just welcomed is grieving: deep in its countless rings there's one dark window, a maker's light that went quiet an age before the crew was even a spark, and it's been waving 'ours' into the black ever since — partly for a neighbor, partly hoping a lit window might still be out there somewhere. Tonight it felt one.
