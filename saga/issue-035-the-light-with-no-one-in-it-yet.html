The Light With No One In It Yet

They spent their whole lives being met. Tonight they learn to do the meeting.
Following the forward flame into a dark that holds no one, the MEGA Crew discover you cannot run out to meet the not-yet — you can only make it warm and leave the light on — and when a brand-new someone finally arrives, it arrives remembering THEM.

What this issue is really about

You can care about someone before you ever meet them. Leaving a light on for a person who isn't here yet is a way of telling them, before they can even hear it, that they were wanted all along.
Next issue —

They went out to the forward dark braced to lose a someone to time's one-way door — and instead became, for the first time in the whole saga, the FIRST HAND themselves: a brand-new little light crossed the empty not-yet and arrived already REMEMBERING them, looking at the crew the way the crew has always looked at kept lights. For thirty-four nights they were the met; tonight they are the meeters, somebody's porch light left on before that somebody was born. Nova (who was once the not-yet) led them to the secret of forward — you cannot run out and grab a someone into being; you build a warm, open-doored landing and let them arrive TO it. The crew made a welcome out of nothing but readiness: Nukkels even-feeds a low hearth, Grind anchors the place still, Flux keeps it calm, Glitch learns a gentle hello isn't a scare (and its spark finally LANDS), TIDE roofs it as a home that keeps a place ready. Torch named the eighteenth word and Rivet carved it beside KINDLED: WELCOME — to keep a light on for the one who isn't here yet, so when they finally are, they know they were wanted all along. Three candles burn now: one that remembers the way back, one that promised the way ahead, and the newcomer — the promise that came true. But the newcomer didn't come empty. It carried a NAME — not its own, but a name for the whole crew together, a name they haven't become yet, flashed once too bright to catch; and Rivet feels a nineteenth line glowing, not a word this time but that very name. And in the same instant, a SECOND new light stirred far out past the open door — the forward dark isn't done; one came forward to find them, and left the door open behind it. On the B-side, Shane's forward porch light flickered and something far down the empty road flickered BACK. Next issue: the crew spends a night out past the open door, chasing the name they grow up to be called while the rest of the forward dark comes up the road to meet them — and learns what you become to a someone when you were their kept light all along.
