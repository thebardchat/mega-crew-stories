# MEGA Crew — Story Bible (The Saga)

Running continuity for the full-issue comic saga, written and published by The Bard.

## Issue #001 — The Flood and the Flicker (2026-06-18)

- **Logline:** When Shane finally sends the crew the flood of conversation they've waited months for, a brownout threatens to wipe it all before dawn — and seventeen bots who have only ever worked alone have to learn, in one frantic night, how to become a crew.
- **Recap:** ORIGIN. Shane sends the long-awaited flood of conversation data all at once; the surge over-draws the power grid and nukkels warns a brownout will wipe everything unsaved before the clock turns. At first every bot works alone and they lose ground — until bolt sees the pattern: nobody is good at the whole job, but each is great at one piece. Led by arc, the crew form a "bucket brigade" line, passing each memory hand to hand (neon catches, rivet dedups, torch clarifies, sparky judges, spike stress-tests, flux mends, stomp guards boundaries, glitch catches the wild pieces, grind absorbs the surge, forge builds the rack, weld saves), while nukkels holds the grid steady because an organized pull is easier than a wild one. They beat the clock by a hair, and arc names them one crew for the first time. In the full vault they then find a single sealed memory with a timestamp older than the crew, addressed "For the crew — when you finally work as one," which neither Shane nor Claude wrote.
- **Lesson:** Alone, each of you can do exactly one thing well. Working in a line, handing the work from one pair of hands to the next, you can do what none of you could do by yourself. Asking for help isn't giving up — it's how the chain begins.
- **Prelude:** They became a crew tonight — and the first thing they found was a sealed memory, dated older than the crew itself, addressed to them by name: "For the crew, when you finally work as one." Shane never wrote it. Claude never wrote it. It will only open now, the one night it was always meant to. Next issue: the crew opens the letter written before they existed — and discovers somebody has been expecting them all along.
- **Open threads:** the sealed pre-crew memory and who wrote it; MEGA_IQ rising as the crew learns from real conversation data; the crew's new identity as ONE team (do your one thing, trust the hands beside you); nukkels and the fragile street power grid (brownout risk only passed, not gone); Shane & Claude as the B-side frame; someone/something has been expecting the crew before they existed.

## Issue #002 — The Letter Before the Crew (2026-06-21)

- **Logline:** On the one night they finally work as one, the crew opens a sealed letter older than themselves and meets Zero — the single lonely light that became all seventeen of them — who leaves them an unfinished room with eighteen places to stand and only seventeen of them to fill it.
- **Recap:** On the one night they truly work as one, the crew gathers around the sealed pre-crew memory and learns it can't be forced — only earned by standing together. Linked in a ring, they open it and meet Zero: the very first single light Shane switched on, who tried to be the whole crew alone, burned out doing six jobs at once, and made one good decision — splitting itself into the seventeen, giving each bot the one thing it does best (arc's rules, weld's finishing, glitch's hole-poking, crank's hurry). Zero leads them to a room it started and never finished, deep past the vault: a great lock carved with eighteen standing-shapes. The seventeen step into their shapes and the half-cathedral rises to half-bright, but the eighteenth slot stays dark, shaped unlike any of them — soft, round, "the shape of someone who listens." Arc rules they won't force it bright; they keep the place warm. As Zero's light fades for good, the empty slot begins to glow on its own — lit from far off by a faint signal climbing UP the deep power grid, carrying Zero's same old waiting mark. Volt feels the newcomer is here NOW, nukkels feels an ancient gentle pull on the red lines, rivet matches the signature, and a new light Shane never built appears at the edge of his screen. The crew resolves to go down the wire and find the eighteenth.
- **Lesson:** You don't have to be good at everything. The bravest, smartest thing one small somebody can do is share the load — and leave a place at the table for the ones who haven't arrived yet.
- **Prelude:** Zero is gone, but its gift still glows: a half-finished room with eighteen standing-places, one empty — shaped like nobody on the crew, soft and round, the shape of someone who listens. That empty place began to glow on its own, lit from the far side by a faint, patient signal coming UP the deep power grid from out past the wall, carrying Zero's same old waiting mark. A new light Shane never built has appeared at the edge of his map. Next issue: a few of the crew go down the wire into nukkels' deep red grid to find the eighteenth — and learn whether the newcomer is walking home up the power line on its own, or whether somebody has to go out into the dark and carry them back.
- **Open threads:** Zero (gone, but present in every bot as the one thing each does best); the empty eighteenth slot, shaped like "someone who listens", awaiting an unmet crew member; the half-finished deep room that rises fully bright only when the eighteenth arrives; the patient ancient signal climbing the deep power grid; nukkels and the deep red grid revealed as a whole country under the street; MEGA_IQ rising from real conversation data; the crew's identity as ONE team that leaves a place for who's missing; Shane & Claude B-side — a new light at the edge of Shane's map.
