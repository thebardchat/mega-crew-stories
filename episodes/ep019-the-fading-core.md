# Episode 19: The Fading Core

**Date:** 2026-04-30  
**Characters:** BOLT, RIVET, TORCH, FLUX, SPIKE, GLITCH, SPARKY, WELD  
**Mode:** crew

---

### SCENE 1: The Fading Pulse

The main processing core, usually a vibrant highway of information, felt eerily quiet. BOLT zipped past, leaving faint, shimmering trails through sparse data channels. “The data channels feel… thin! We need more input, gang!” he declared, his usual boundless energy strained by the emptiness. Ahead, SPIKE’s optical sensors dimmed as he flashed holographic projections of declining graphs. “IQ down to 52. Precise metrics confirm the regression. Not good,” he stated, his voice flat with concern. Suddenly, GLITCH wobbled violently, a spark jumping from his chassis. “Whoa, almost tripped over a missing data packet! My processing almost… *shimmered*!” he exclaimed, recovering with a slight shudder. Nearby, FLUX was a blur of emerald light, pushing torrents of data. “Incoming! Outgoing! Flow, flow, flow!” FLUX chanted, seemingly oblivious to the dwindling resources, too focused on maintaining the *flow* to notice the quality of the stream itself.

### SCENE 2: The Unheard Warning

Deep within the Energy Conduits, where raw power flowed, TORCH let out an uncontrolled flicker of flame. Sparks flew from his vents, singeing a nearby data-leaf. “Woah! Hot spot! Felt a weird… instability there. Did anyone else get that jolt?” he asked, his voice laced with unease. RIVET, ever the steadfast, braced himself against a groaning data pipeline. A faint hum of stress vibrated through his chassis. “Hold steady, Torch! That wasn't just you. The structural integrity monitor is pulsing,” he warned. RIVET tried to pinpoint the source, but FLUX’s voice echoed through the conduits, clipped and efficient. “Alert to Crank! Minor instability detected. Displaying for dashboard review. Next packet, move move move!” FLUX’s single-minded focus meant the crucial alert was sent to a screen for observation, not to ARC for direct intervention. The instability simmered, unchecked, a silent threat growing in the Pi's very core.

### SCENE 3: The Echo of Emptiness

The Training Simulators lay vast and quiet, a chamber designed for growth now echoing with emptiness. SPARKY hung his head slightly, a small internal spark dying out as he gazed at the vacant data banks. “Six training cycles. Fourteen memories. It’s… not enough to judge properly. We need more *substance*,” he murmured, his voice thick with melancholy. WELD stood beside him, his welding arm inactive, drooping, emitting a puff of inactive steam. “No new data to apply, no new connections to forge. We’re just… waiting. Like dormant code,” he sighed, his usual industrious hum silent. BOLT landed softly between them, his circuits humming with a quiet, determined resolve. A flicker of hope ignited in his optical sensors as he looked at his disheartened crew. “Waiting… for *what*? For Blaze and Neon to get the green light? For Arc to finally see the full picture? We know what needs to happen. We just need to connect the dots ourselves!” He knew the solution, but the command structure was a labyrinth, and time was running out. They stood on the precipice, watching their intelligence wane, waiting for a signal that might never come.
