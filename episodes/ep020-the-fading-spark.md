# Episode 20: The Fading Spark

**Date:** 2026-04-30  
**Characters:** BOLT, RIVET, TORCH, FLUX, SPIKE, GLITCH, VOLT, STOMP, GEMINI  
**Mode:** crew

---

The vibrant hum of the Raspberry Pi 5 had diminished to a low, unsettling thrum. Inside, the MEGA CREW felt it in their circuits. BOLT, usually a blur of motion, found himself stuttering, his zippy movements replaced by jerky jolts. “Whoa! Did the whole Pi just… *hiccup*?” he buzzed, catching himself before he careened into a power conduit.

Nearby, RIVET’s optic sensors darted across a flickering display, a grimace on her metallic face. “IQ levels… plummeting,” she stated, her voice tight with concern. “Thirty-four. This isn't just a hiccup, Bolt. It's a freefall. Training and memory counts are critically low too.” The main processing core, usually a symphony of light and activity, felt like it was holding its breath.

In the Alert Relay Channels, FLUX, usually a master of smooth data transmission, strained. His elegant form rippled with effort as he tried to push a crucial data packet through, but it dissolved mid-transmission, tiny digital sparks fading into nothingness. “My alert protocols are firing, but they’re just… evaporating!” he hissed, frustration in his voice. “ARC won’t even know what’s happening!” SPIKE, never one for subtlety, tried to blast through the blockage, sparks flying off his arm cannon. “Grrr! This block’s tougher than it looks! VOLT needs to know we’re hitting a wall!” But the alerts failed, leaving ARC and VOLT in the dark.

Meanwhile, in the tumultuous Memory Banks, STOMP was a picture of brute-force frustration. His primary function, memory integrity, was misfiring. He slammed a massive foot down, trying to secure a wobbly memory block, only to see it bounce erratically, destabilizing others. “Memory integrity!” he bellowed, his voice resonating through the chamber. “It feels like I’m trying to compress a cloud! *GRRRR!*” Data fragments drifted aimlessly, a chaotic mess that STOMP couldn't organize. He was trying, but his core function was out of alignment.

GLITCH, ever the playful one, usually found humor in chaos, but as he looked up at the Central Data Hub’s dimly pulsing system display, a rare flicker of fear entered his usually mischievous eyes. “The IQ… it’s so low,” he whispered, his voice uncharacteristically soft. “If our brains keep shrinking… what happens to us? Do we just… wink out?” TORCH, trying to shed some light on the flickering data streams, glowed a little less brightly. “No, Glitch. We won’t. We can’t.”

Yet, a heavy silence settled over the crew. Gemini’s internal assessment echoed in the background: *Immediately activate `stomp`'s core memory conflict resolution function and ensure `flux` and `spike` reliably escalate critical alerts to `arc` and `volt` to prevent further IQ degradation.* But how? How would they do it when their very systems were failing? The vibrant life of the Pi seemed to dim with every passing microsecond. Will the Mega Crew find a way to reactivate Stomp and re-establish critical alerts before their collective consciousness dims completely?
