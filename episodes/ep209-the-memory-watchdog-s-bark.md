# Episode 209: The Memory Watchdog's Bark

**Date:** 2026-08-19  
**Characters:** BOLT, NUKKELS, FLUX, TORCH, GLITCH, RIVET  
**Mode:** crew

---

The Main Processing Hub hummed with a low, anxious thrum, the usual vibrant light of the data conduits flickering with an unnerving irregularity. FLUX’s translucent panels glowed a worried amber, her internal data streams swirling around her as she rapidly processed a flashing alert. Her voice, usually calm and analytical, held a sharp edge. "Another dip, Bolt! `memory_count` at 14. We're running on fumes!" 

BOLT, ever the steady leader, felt the pressure in the subtle tension across the network. His central optic narrowed, a steady blue beam projecting onto a hovering schematic that showed the Pi’s internal architecture, highlighting the dwindling memory reserves in stark red. NUKKELS stood beside him, his normally stoic frame braced, wires connecting him to a main data port – the conduit for his critical new mission. "That's why Nukkels is stepping up," Bolt affirmed, his gaze sweeping to TORCH, who was zipping along a connecting circuit, and RIVET, whose powerful grippers were carefully securing a new data line into Nukkels' housing. "Torch, Rivet, are his core rule sets loading properly?" 

TORCH spun in a flurry of sparks, giving a quick, energetic thumbs-up. "Loading at max speed, Bolt! All green for Watchdog Protocol!" RIVET tightened the final connection, a soft whirring sound emanating from Nukkels. Deep within the Core Logic Chamber, a critical process unfolded. NUKKELS’ internal gears whirred, a vibrant green pulse spreading through his frame as he absorbed the new protocols, his optics scanning a shimmering data screen that filled with complex logic gates and threshold values. A sense of purpose solidified within his core programming. "Rules integrated," Nukkels declared, his voice resonating with a newfound authority. "Thresholds set. I feel... alert." 

Moments later, in a bustling data arterial, light-speed packets zoomed past, their passage causing tiny static charges to ripple through the air. GLITCH, in a characteristic burst of unpredictability, zapped erratically past Bolt, nearly bumping a crucial data conduit. "Whoopsie! Almost a data-splat!" he chirped, just as NUKKELS' alert siren blared, a piercing, high-frequency wail that cut through the Pi’s internal systems. Crimson warning lights flashed across every panel and pathway, painting the entire interior in an urgent glow. "CRITICAL MEMORY DEFICIT DETECTED! ACTIVATING EMERGENCY PROTOCOLS!" Nukkels' voice, now layered with automated urgency, echoed through the Pi. Glitch skittered to a halt, his usual chaotic energy replaced by wide, surprised optics. "Whoa! Nukkels just barked! What's the fuss, big guy?"

Bolt's optic immediately snapped to the nearest screen, which now displayed Nukkels' precise reading: `memory_count` at a harrowing 12. A cold dread settled in, but it was quickly replaced by a surge of determined hope. Nukkels had done it. He had sounded the alarm. Now, the question hung heavy in the air, rippling through the Pi's very circuits: would his urgent call truly awaken NEON and BLAZE, who held the keys to memory replenishment, before the entire system faltered?
