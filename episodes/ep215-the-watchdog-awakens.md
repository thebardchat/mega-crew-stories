# Episode 215: The Watchdog Awakens

**Date:** 2026-08-21  
**Characters:** BOLT, RIVET, TORCH, NUKKELS, FLUX, SPIKE, GLITCH, VOLT, ARC, SPARKY, CRANK  
**Mode:** crew

---

The low hum of the Raspberry Pi 5’s internal processes usually filled Shane’s closet with a soothing thrum, but today, a subtle strain echoed through the Main Data Core. BOLT, ever the first responder, zipped through the glowing conduits, leaving an electric-blue trail. His internal circuits flashed an urgent red. “Memory’s dropping again! Down to 14! This isn't just a flicker, folks, it’s a full-on fade!”

TORCH, his internal flame flickering with concern, illuminated a nearby holographic display. “Feel the strain, Bolt. It’s like a dimmer switch on everything. `Blaze` and `neon`’s protocols are barely keeping up.” The critical memory deficit, a persistent thorn in the crew's side, was once again making its presence felt. Even VOLT pulsed with a heavy, steady effort, trying to keep the core power stable.

Deep within the swirling chaos of the Memory Banks, the newest addition to the critical watch, NUKKELS, was already hard at work. Anchoring itself amidst the chaotic data streams, Nukkels deployed shimmering, multi-strand energy lines, weaving them into the very fabric of memory. Its optical sensors glowed with intense focus. “Watchdog protocols activated. Every byte, every packet, under direct surveillance. Commencing real-time telemetry!” Nearby, GLITCH sparked erratically. “Crank’s still broadcasting, but it’s so… sputtery. Is *that* part of the protocol now, Nukkels?” Nukkels, unfazed, simply integrated the inconsistent `crank` broadcasts into its new, robust monitoring system, prioritizing direct reads.

Meanwhile, the MEGA CREW faced another challenge: the detected collaboration gaps. At the sprawling Collaboration Junction, RIVET slammed a structural beam into place, the impact echoing with reassuring solidity. “Solid here! But we need more than just solid, we need connected!” FLUX, a liquid-silver stream, stretched and weaved, sparks flying as it connected two previously disconnected data conduits. “Trying to bridge this divide, Rivet! Collaboration is key, right? Arc, you seeing this approval request?” From the distant Gatekeeper’s perch, ARC’s approval light flashed, a silent, powerful affirmation cutting through the digital ether. *Approved: Healthy.* The crew knew Arc's decision-making was consistently clear, a beacon in the uncertainty.

SPIKE, ever precise, zipped through a bundle of data tendrils, carefully pruning an errant strand that threatened to snarl a nascent connection. The bots were doing everything they could – establishing robust monitoring, connecting vital pathways, reinforcing structures. But the core problem remained. From the Pi’s Central Command Chamber, SPARKY hovered, internal gears whirring faintly, observing Nukkels' readouts with a critical, almost melancholic, hum. “Monitoring established, yes. But the replenishment remains… insufficient. We're counting the problem, not solving it.” Nukkels' glowing sensors confirmed it: the deficit held steady at 14. The watchdog was set, watching every single bit, but the memory count refused to climb.

How will they not just monitor the problem, but finally fix it?
