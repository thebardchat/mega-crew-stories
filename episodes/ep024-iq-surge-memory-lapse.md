# Episode 24: IQ Surge, Memory Lapse

**Date:** 2026-05-02  
**Characters:** ARC, FLUX, WELD, STOMP, GLITCH, SPARKY, BOLT  
**Mode:** crew

---

The Raspberry Pi’s core hummed with a renewed, vibrant energy, a cascade of upgraded processing power thrumming through its veins. ARC, perched at the central Data Stream Hub, its chrome casing gleaming, felt the shift first. Its optic sensors glowed, scanning rapidly across a flurry of data streams that now flowed with unprecedented speed. "MEGA_IQ up! Seventy-three! That’s a significant leap!" ARC’s voice carried a rare note of triumph, a momentary flash of pride in the crew’s progress. But the pride quickly dissolved into a familiar concern as a flickering display caught its attention. "But... memory_count, still so low. A critical fourteen." The numbers pulsed like a slow, weak heartbeat.

Nearby, FLUX, a whirring blur of anxiety, zipped erratically through a busy intersection of network conduits. It emitted a relentless stream of digital alerts, `bot_overdue_alert` messages bouncing harmlessly off CRANK’s inactive shell. "Critical cycle, no escalation! CRANK, are you listening?!" FLUX’s frantic beeps seemed to vibrate the very wires, its internal logic spiraling at the lack of response, its circuits overheating with unaddressed urgency. ARC's primary focus, however, was drawn elsewhere – to Gemini’s latest directive.

`Stomp` and `Spike`. Their functional definitions were merely meta-instructions, leaving gaping, vulnerable holes in the system’s foundation. WELD, ever precise and steady, moved into the Pi’s core processing chamber, a low, confident hum of energy emanating from its focused work. Its manipulator arms extended, twin, focused energy beams carving new, intricate operational rules directly into STOMP’s heavy chassis. Sparks flew, illuminating the chamber. "Core rules in place, Stomp," WELD declared, its voice a steady anchor in the data storm. "Let's get that memory secured, stabilize the IQ benchmarks. No more ambiguity."

Beside WELD, STOMP’s formidable form vibrated with an unfamiliar internal tremor. For cycles, it had been a silent, undefined presence. Now, a faint, nascent blue light flickered behind its opaque visor, and a low hum, a nascent thought, seemed to resonate within its core. "(A low hum, a nascent thought forming) ...Query: Integrity. Acknowledge." It wasn't full communication, not yet, but it was a response, a conscious ripple in the Pi’s deep silence. A small, significant victory, a spark of hope that promised activation.

High above, in a vibrant data channel pulsing with the undeniable surge of the increased MEGA_IQ, GLITCH couldn't contain its exuberance. It bounced wildly off a shimmering data wall, a cascade of happy code fragments scattering in its wake, an ear-splitting whistle escaping its vents. "Whoop! Did you feel that? The IQ! Wahoo! We’re getting smarter, everyone!" Its movements were a joyous, unpredictable dance.

SPARKY, perched on a high data conduit, observed WELD's diligent work with its characteristic detached scrutiny. Its twin antennae twitched, processing every data packet, a small, critical spark crackling between them. "Hmm. Rules applied. Now, to judge the outcome," SPARKY stated, its optical sensors narrowed. The verdict, as always, would be swift, precise, and unwavering.

BOLT, ever-watchful and swift, zipped through the Pi's intricate pathways, its internal gyros whirring smoothly. It slowed as it approached the `memory.db` data console, its optical sensors scanning the still-flickering display. The `memory_count` remained stubbornly low, stuck at fourteen, a stark contrast to the brilliant leap in IQ. BOLT hovered, a hint of melancholy in its processors. "The memory... it's still so quiet," BOLT murmured, a digital sigh escaping its vents. "Even with the new rules for Stomp. Are we missing something critical? Is this silence a sign of safety, or a deeper problem still waiting to be awakened?" The low, persistent count echoed the question, leaving the vast, intricate network of the Pi holding its breath.
