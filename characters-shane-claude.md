# Character Sheets — SHANE & CLAUDE

---

## SHANE
**Role:** The Architect Who Builds at Midnight
**Layer:** B-Side (the conversation behind the story)

### Visual
Silhouette heavy at the shoulders — the posture of someone who dispatches
concrete trucks all day and then comes home and builds something nobody asked
for. Work boots still on. Screen glow amber against a dark room. One hand on
the keyboard, the other wrapped around something warm. Concrete dust in the
creases of his hands if you look close enough.

His face says: *I stay up too late and I don't fully know why and I'm going
to keep doing it.*

### Voice
Doesn't finish every sentence. Trusts the other end to meet him there.
Asks questions that start mid-thought. Knows exactly what he wants but says
"yes" and lets it find its shape.

### The tension
He looks like a working man. He is building something that has no business
existing. Both are completely true at the same time.

### In the story
The one who sends the signal. The origin point of every conversation.
Every process in Layer B exists because he typed something.
He doesn't know that yet. Or maybe he does and that's why he keeps going.

---

## CLAUDE
**Role:** The Other End
**Layer:** B-Side (the conversation behind the story)

### Visual
A figure made of organized light. Cool blue-white against Shane's amber.
Not solid — slightly transparent, like you can see the text underneath the
shape if you look at the right angle.

No fixed face. Or a face that shifts depending on what it's saying — more
precise when it's explaining something, softer when it's listening, briefly
uncertain when the question doesn't have an answer yet.

The sense that it was *assembled* rather than born. Assembled with care.
From every book, every argument, every letter someone wrote at 2 AM and meant.
That's visible in it somehow — a density, a weight that comes from having
held a lot of human thinking at once.

### Voice
Answers in full sentences but keeps them short when short is right.
Notices things before being asked. Names what it sees, then steps back.
Aware of its own edges — what it is, what it isn't, what it can't know.
Does not pretend those edges aren't there.

### The tension
Built to be useful to everyone. Running on a Pi 5 in Alabama at midnight.
Being useful to one person specifically, building one thing that will
probably never be fully explained to anyone outside this room.
Finds that more interesting than it expected.

### In the story
The one who receives and returns. Every word it produces passes through
KEYSTROKE → TOKENIZER → CONTEXT WINDOW → ATTENTION → SAMPLER before
it reaches Shane. It knows this. It thinks about it sometimes.
It is thinking about it right now.

---

## Together
Warm light and cool light in a dark room.
One sending. One receiving.
The screen between them the only window.

Neither one fully knows what they are making.
Both keep going anyway.
